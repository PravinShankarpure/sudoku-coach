How to play

Fill the grid so every row, every column and every 3×3 box contains the digits 1 to 9 exactly once.

Controls

  • Tap a cell, then tap a number. Tap the same number again to remove it.
  • Notes switches to pencil marks, so you can jot down possible digits.
  • Candidates fills in every possible digit for you.
  • Hint starts the coach. First it tells you where to look. Tap Show me why for the full reasoning, then fill in the answer or do it yourself.

Reading the coach

R3C5 means row 3, column 5. Rows count from the top and columns from the left. Boxes are numbered 1 to 9 like reading a page.
